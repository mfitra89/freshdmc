Cancellation Policy

Cancellation and refund eligibility depends on how far in advance the booking is cancelled and on supplier-specific conditions displayed at checkout.

Customer Cancellation

  • More than 72 hours before service: normally eligible for the highest available refund, less non-refundable payment or supplier charges where disclosed.
  • 24–72 hours before service: partial cancellation charges may apply.
  • Less than 24 hours before service or no-show: booking may be non-refundable.

Flight Delays

Where flight monitoring is included, the provider will make reasonable efforts to adjust pickup time. Significant changes may require reconfirmation.

Provider Cancellation

If a confirmed provider cannot perform the service, Fresh Destination will attempt to arrange a suitable replacement. If no replacement can be arranged, the affected service will be refunded.

Changes to Bookings

Date, pickup time, route, passenger count or vehicle changes are subject to availability and may change the price.

Refund Timing

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method. Bank and card processing times vary.

How to Cancel

Use the booking management link in your confirmation email or contact Fresh Destination support and quote your booking reference.
